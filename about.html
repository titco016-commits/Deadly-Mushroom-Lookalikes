Mission

Created for viewers who love to learn neat facts about one of natures biggest wonders, dedicated to informing individuals of the edible plants and mushrooms found on our local land and executed with safety in mind.

Our goal is to encourage people to become self sufficient, and understand how to use the nature around them safely. We hope to educate on the dangers of misidentification and the importance of proper foraging techniques. Please remember to consult with an expert before consuming any wild plants or mushrooms, while we love to learn about mushrooms too, this is not a substitute for professional advice.
